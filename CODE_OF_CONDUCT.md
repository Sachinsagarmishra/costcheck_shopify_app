# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CostCheck to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, identity, or where in the world they live. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for everybody.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially with beginners.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before judging.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **hi@sachindesign.com**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does.
